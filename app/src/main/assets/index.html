APP GUIDE

Why This App Was Made

This app is made to make daily petrol pump data collection simple, fast and structured. Instead of depending on scattered messages or manual notes, the pump team can enter important daily information in one place.

How it helps pump management• Gives the owner timely information about daily fuel sales, stock variation, collections and settlements.• Keeps Full Day, Evening and Settlement information organized.• Makes employee present/absent information available in the same report.• Creates a ready WhatsApp report so information can be shared quickly with the owner.• Keeps saved records on the device for checking, editing and correcting later.

How to Use

1. Full DayEnter MS/HSD readings, collection and employee attendance.
2. EveningEnter evening sales, collection, cash handover and attendance.
3. SettlementEnter PhonePe, ATM and DT Plus settlement details.
4. SaveSave records locally on this device. Edit or delete them from History.
5. WhatsAppCheck the live preview, then tap WhatsApp Send to share the report.
Employee names are shared between Full Day and Evening. Theme can be changed with the ☀ / ☾ switch.
